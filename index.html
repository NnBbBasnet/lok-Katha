Where it all begins

Every celebration has a story.

Somewhere along the way, those stories grew quiet. Here’s why Lok-katha began.

A festival is more than a day on the calendar. It is a story, a memory, a piece of who we are.

The festivals we love

Dashain, Tihar, Lhosar, Maghe Sankranti—each year we gather, celebrate, and carry on traditions that connect us.

But do our children know why?

The meaning behind the rituals can be hard to find. Many parents are still searching for those stories, too.

The storytellers live far away

Grandparents once gathered children close and told the old tales. Today, families often live apart, and the chain of sharing has grown quieter.

A storybook-shaped gap

Adult books hold pieces of our heritage, yet little readers have few picture books to help them discover what a celebration means.
